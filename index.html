What else your employer may ask for

  • Your HRA declaration: Form 124 from April 2026 (Form 12BB before that).
  • A rent agreement.
  • The owner’s PAN, if rent is over ₹1 lakh in a financial year.
  • Bank statements, if you paid online.

Rules change; for the latest, see the Income Tax Department’s portal or ask your employer.

How it works

  1. Fill in the blanks on the receipt: your name, the monthly rent, the house address and the owner. Moved or your rent changed? Add another stay.
  2. Choose one receipt a month or one a quarter, and add a signature line and stamp box if you need them.
  3. Generate and download one PDF with every receipt in date order. Print it and have the owner sign each one.
